A proper inspection in about 20 minutes

No clipboard, no guessing what to photograph. The app knows which shots each room needs.

  1. Walk each room

    Follow the checklist: walls, floor, windows, fixtures. Meters, keys and smoke alarms get their own step.

  2. Note what's wrong

    Spot a scuff or a crack? Tap Issue, take the photo and describe it by typing or speaking.

  3. Send the report

    Sign, then send the PDF by email or WhatsApp. Your sent email becomes an independent date.

A report your landlord can't wave away

Built to be read by a landlord, an agent or a deposit scheme adjudicator.

  • Every photo fingerprintedA SHA-256 fingerprint is taken the moment each photo is captured, so any later change shows.
  • Dated and referencedEach page is dated, and each photo has a reference number and caption.
  • Move-out, side by sideAt move-out, line up each shot over your move-in photo and link old issues to new ones.
  • For both sidesLandlords and agents can use it too: the wording switches to their side.

Everything stays on your iPhone

No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Photos, notes and dictation stay on your phone until you choose to send the report. It works offline, so an empty flat with no signal is fine. Back up to Files or iCloud Drive whenever you like.
