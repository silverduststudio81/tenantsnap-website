Every room has its checklist

No clipboard, no guessing what to photograph. Pick your rooms and TenantSnap lists the shots each one needs.

Add, rename or remove rooms to match your home. Already living there? Start any time with a condition check: a dated record of the home as it is today.

A report your landlord can't wave away

Built to be read by a landlord, an agent or a deposit scheme adjudicator.

Every photo fingerprinted
A SHA-256 fingerprint is taken the moment each photo is captured, so any later change shows.
Dated and referenced
Each page is dated, and each photo has a reference number and caption.
Move-out, side by side
At move-out, line up each shot over your move-in photo and link old issues to new ones.
Repairs, on the record
Something breaks? Log it with dated photos and send a problem report. Your move-out report lists them all.
For both sides
Landlords and agents can use it too: the wording switches to their side.

From the day you move in to the day you leave

Everything stays on your phone

No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Photos, notes and dictation stay on your phone until you choose to send the report. It works offline, so an empty flat with no signal is fine. Back up whenever you like: to Files or iCloud Drive on iPhone, to Google Drive or any folder on Android.
